Syeda Qadri, M.D.

Board Certified Family Physician

Dr. Qadri was born in New York, and on receiving a scholarship she earned her medical degree from Dow Medical College. She later completed her residency in family practice at Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center. Dr. Qadri is board-certified in family medicine and provides compassionate care towards her patients. She practices urgent care and women's health care, providing a wide range of care, and has been in practice since 2011. She is fluent in Urdu and Hindi. She is married and practices with her husband; in her leisure time she likes to read.

Sarah Zaidi, PA-C

Physician Assistant

Sarah Zaidi is a board-certified Physician Assistant with a strong educational and professional background in healthcare. She completed her Physician Assistant training at West Coast University Richardson, where she developed the clinical knowledge and hands-on skills necessary to provide high-quality patient care. Prior to her graduate training, she earned her undergraduate degree from the University of Texas at Dallas, building a solid academic foundation that supported her pursuit of a career in medicine.

Hassan Imam

Board Certified Family Physician

Dr. Imam is a Board Certified Family Medicine physician. He completed his training at Texas Tech University Amarillo in 2006. He practices in urgent and primary care clinics located in Murphy and Richardson, providing a wide range of treatment options for his patients. He has served as a hospitalist, primary care and urgent care provider, and even had the privilege to work at Texas Tech University as an attending physician. In his spare time he likes to spend time with family; he also enjoys swimming and painting. He speaks Hindi, Punjabi and Urdu.

Subrien Almahrooq, PA-C

Physician Assistant

Subrien Almahrooq is a board certified Physician Assistant. Subrien is licensed to practice in the state of Texas within the scope of medicine. She completed her training at University of Texas Medical Branch and undergraduate degree at University of North Texas. In her free time, she likes to travel, explore new places and play sports. She is fluent in Arabic.
