What primary care covers

Urgent care handles today’s problem. Primary care helps you stay ahead of the next one. Our board-certified family physicians see patients for primary care at both clinics, and our providers are accepting new patients.

Establishing care

  • Meet a physician who gets to know you and your history
  • Review of your medical history and current medications
  • A plan for the care you need going forward

Ongoing & chronic conditions

  • Asthma
  • Diabetes
  • High blood pressure
  • High cholesterol

Check-ups & physicals

  • Annual check-ups
  • Physical exams
  • Preventive care conversations about what is right for you

Follow-up care

  • Follow-up after an urgent care visit
  • The same physicians who staff our urgent care
  • Care at either Murphy or Little Elm

Booking a primary care visit

Primary care visits are scheduled — call (972) 442-4700 and tell us whether Murphy or Little Elm is closer to you. Clinic hours differ by location, so see the Murphy and Little Elm pages before you plan your visit. If you need care today, urgent care is available at both clinics.

This page is general information, not medical advice. For a life-threatening emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

Primary care FAQs

What is the difference between primary care and urgent care?

Urgent care is for problems that need attention today. Primary care is an ongoing relationship with a physician who manages long-term conditions, does check-ups, and helps you plan preventive care. You can use both at MedStar Family Care, with the same physicians.

How do I become a new primary care patient?

Call (972) 442-4700 to schedule your first visit at Murphy or Little Elm. You can fill out the new patient forms ahead of time from our patient information page.

Can I follow up with a primary care physician after an urgent care visit?

Yes. Because our urgent care and primary care physicians are the same team, you can schedule follow-up care after an urgent care visit. Call (972) 442-4700 to book.
