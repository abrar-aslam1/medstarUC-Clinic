Why work here

The basics, done right.

We pay competitively and protect your time off. The rest of what makes us different is harder to put in a benefits brochure — but it's why people stay.

01 / TIME

Real visit lengths

We book longer visits on purpose. You'll have time to actually listen to a patient instead of watching the clock.

02 / TEAM

No corporate layer

No regional VP. No quarterly RVU pressure. Decisions about care, staffing, and scheduling get made in the building, by people who see patients themselves.

03 / GROWTH

Room to grow

CME budget, conference time, and mentorship from senior physicians. We support certifications, fellowships, and clinical interests outside of bread-and-butter primary care.

04 / BENEFITS

Competitive benefits

We offer a competitive benefits package. Ask our practice manager for the specifics when you apply.

05 / SCHEDULE

Predictable hours

Clinic-based hours, no overnight call, and PTO that actually gets taken — we cover for each other.

06 / CULTURE

A clinic, not a chain

We eat lunch together. We know each other's kids' names. New hires get a real onboarding, not a slide deck.
